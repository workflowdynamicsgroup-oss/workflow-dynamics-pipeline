# Phase 4: script rules

`config.json` is authoritative for every number. This file names the limits (config keys in backticks) and does not restate their values. The critical rules (R-HONESTY, R-DISC, R-NOSPOKEN, R-NOFAKE, R-NOPROPS, R-AIFLAG, R-SPEC, R-SHOTS, R-GENLIMIT, R-AVATAR, R-CLOSE, R-FACTS) are worded once in SKILL.md; where this file repeats one it adds detail only. Write all LINE content and shots into the source file first. Code computes words, durations and timestamps. Under the word band: deepen category scenes, cost math or the objection beat; never pad.

## Length and structure

- **R-LENGTH.** Runtime must reach `runtime_min_s` (the hard floor) and aims at `runtime_target_s`. `runtime_max_s` is a rough ceiling: over it is a warning, not a block. The word band is those runtimes at the configured wpm (`defaults.wpm`). If you choose to trim past the ceiling: cut the weakest category, tighten explanation LINEs, shorten avatar blocks. Never cut the objection beat, held reveal, risk reversal, a segment verdict or a comparison card.
- **R-SECRET (hybrid).** Early: segment map (one line per segment) inside `segment_map_window_s`. Held: one verified number that flips a segment's verdict (usually the total-cost gap) is teased without its value and revealed inside `held_reveal_window_s`. If no number flips a verdict, hold the largest verified hidden-cost finding and say so at the gate. Every chapter ends with a tease pointing to a specific later payoff. The held number must not appear before the reveal. These windows are absolute times in seconds from the start, so check them after any change to scene length.
- **R-OPEN.** Hook LINE is within `hook_line_max_words`, lands inside `hook_slam_window_s`, carries one concrete number, price or named feature. Its key word slams on screen with a snap animation. At least `hook_min_visual_changes` visual changes in that window. Opening shot is the generated hook clip (`GEN-VIDEO`) when the cap allows. The avatar enters on the next scene (clip-first opener). Never a greeting, logo or channel welcome.
- **R-CLOSE.** Two windows, both enforced by lint. (1) The end-screen window: exactly one scene carries `end_screen_start`, placed `end_screen_lead_s` before the end of the video; from there the left and right thirds stay quiet for YouTube end-screen elements. (2) The final scene is section `end_screen`, runs at least `end_screen_min_s`, and is built from `GEN-VIDEO` or `B-ROLL` shots only. Peak in the final second, focal point centered. Its spoken line is within `closing_line_max_words`, one ask, echoing the hook.
- **R-NARRATION.** Chatterbox input must read correctly with no human edit. Spoken LINE text and `[BEAT]` only. `to_narration()` converts numbers, currency and percentages to words and applies the pronunciation map; do not hand-convert. Write numbers as digits in LINEs and on-screen text. Every product name needs a pronunciation entry (`config.json`, `pronunciations`). Run the 60-second Chatterbox test before the full render and mark entries verified.
- **R-CTA.** Verdict buttons: "Start {product} with my link". Offer button: "Use my link first". All buttons link to the VS page. Only for products with an affiliate link.
- **R-FACTS.** See SKILL.md. Every field in `intake_required_fields` is filled before the gate; pricing carries its date and source URLs, ratings name the review site, and a TBD blocks the gate.

## Scene sequence (section names are the `section` values)

1. `hook` 0:00-0:45. Hook part 1 pattern interrupt; part 2 the stakes. Speak the primary search phrase once before 1:00.
2. `segment_map` (inside `segment_map_window_s`). One line per segment, both products strong; roadmap tease of the held number. The segment map opens with an `ASSET-BRAND` shot for each product.
3. `micro_authority` about 1:30-1:50, per tier, device per R-VARY.
4. `problem` about 1:50-2:30.
5. `agitation` about 2:30-3:30. Name the cost of inaction (`names_cost_of_inaction`). Required objection beat (`addresses_objection`).
6. Solution: 4-5 `category` pairs (A strength, then B strength ending "this favors X because..."; each ends in a curiosity loop); `soft_cta` spoken only, no button, inside `soft_cta_window_s`; `third_party_proof` 1-2 scenes (rating with review count on screen, never a rating without its count); `pattern_interrupt`; `switching_cost` only if relevant; `pricing` last as sticker price only, ending on a loop.
7. `reveal_bridge` then `held_reveal` (inside `held_reveal_window_s`): both all-in monthly totals as real numbers on screen at the spoken timestamp, and the number that flips a verdict. Never end pricing on a sticker comparison.
8. Close: `verdict` + `verdict_reason` per segment (one clear answer, never hedged, each with its button), `recap` with comment bait ("Comment A or B"), `risk_reversal` (trial, money-back window, card required, cancellation ease; say plainly if terms are weak), `cost_of_inaction`, `offer` (use my link before creating an account; button), `end_screen`.

Honest-segments rule: if one product wins every segment, record `video.single_winner_reason`; otherwise each product must clearly win at least one segment with honest limits.

## LINE and voice (R-VOICE)

LINE: within `line_max_sentences` and `line_max_words`. Only text in the LINE is spoken. Banned phrases and uncontracted forms are in config. Contractions required. No semicolons, no em-dash chains. Every LINE that delivers a stat, price, verdict or hook is `short_line_min_words` to `short_line_max_words` words, one idea. Never two long explanation LINEs back to back; break with a short one every 2-3 scenes. Insert `[BEAT]` where a pause belongs.

## Visual rules: the pipeline's shot types

Every shot carries one of seven tags. They are exactly what the pipeline can render, so a script never plans a visual the pipeline cannot make.

| Tag | What it is | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `GEN-VIDEO` | Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B clip | 2-5 s |
| `GEN-STILL` | FLUX still with a slow Ken Burns push | 9-16 s |
| `B-ROLL` | Pixabay footage found from the shot's `search` nouns, chosen against its Means / Picture brief | 2-10 s |
| `STAT` | Stat card: Value, Label, Source | 9-16 s |
| `ASSET-SHOT` | Real screenshot of a named screen | 2-10 s |
| `ASSET-CLIP` | Real screen recording | 2-10 s |
| `ASSET-BRAND` | Brand-kit logo | 2-5 s |

- **R-SHOTS (critical).** Every scene has at least one shot, and each shot has a tag. Seconds are whole numbers. Wan clips and brand logos run 2-5 s, `B-ROLL` and real captures (`ASSET-SHOT`, `ASSET-CLIP`) run 2-10 s, and a `GEN-STILL` or `STAT` shot runs 9-16 s. A 9-16 s shot is written on the first of the scenes it sits under, with `span: N` (it covers that scene and the next N-1 scenes, which carry no shots of their own). Longer scenes are split into lettered shots sized evenly, each a hard cut to a different image or clearly different framing. A shot timeline that drifts more than 3 s from the narration is a failure. `B-ROLL` stays within `broll_max_share` of the runtime, so the rest comes from comparison cards, real captures, Wan clips and the few stills. Plan that mix while writing, not afterward.
- **R-COMPARE (critical).** Statics sell products; abstract pictures do not. Side-by-side comparison cards carry the visual weight of every video, and they are built as real text in Remotion, never generated. A card is a `STAT` shot with `stat.compare = {kind, a, b, rows}`: `a` is product A (left), `b` is product B, and `rows` holds 2 to `compare_rows_max` entries of `{label, a, b}`. One card for each kind in `compare_kinds`, at least `compare_cards_min` cards in all, and the cards fill at least `compare_share_min` of the runtime (lint checks both; the share scales the count with the video, so a 9-10 minute video lands around 10-12 cards). The kinds: `reviews` (rating sites, with review counts), `pricing` (plan prices in two columns), `monthly_cost` (one stated scenario, both bills and the gap), `features` (what each includes and who it fits), `pros_cons` (rows labeled Pro or Con with each product's own entry, from the research and your hands-on notes) and `pain_points` (each row's `label` is a viewer pain phrase from the Pain-Point Map, kept close to the verbatim wording, and `a` and `b` say how each product handles it). Repeat a kind whenever a scene has its own facts: a second `pricing` or `monthly_cost` card for add-ons and hidden costs, a `features` card for risk reversal (trial, money-back window, card required, cancellation ease), one `features` card per feature group, a `pros_cons` card for each product, and a `pain_points` card for each audience segment. Every scene that states a price, rating, feature set, pro or con, pain point or term shows a card, never a generated picture. Values match `locked_facts` and carry a dated source, like any stat card, and a pain phrase names where it was heard. Card length follows R-SHOTS (9-16 s), so give each card the scenes it spans. When time is short, cut B-roll, stills and Wan clips, never a card.
- **R-BROLL.** Each B-roll shot carries a brief in the form "Means: <the line it supports>. Picture: <one concrete thing>." (10-35 words) and a `search` of one or two plain nouns. The search nouns, not the brief, go into the stock-site search box; the brief is for the person choosing among the clips. The Picture says what is there, with no negation words ("no people" pulls in people), no readable text, no logos and no product UI. A noun is used at most `broll_noun_max_uses` times and never in adjacent shots, and no two adjacent briefs match.
- **R-STAT.** A stat card needs Value, Label and Source. The Source is a named page and date. Numbers match `locked_facts` exactly.
- **R-INSERT.** Real captures only: within `screenshot_cap` and `screen_rec_cap` overall and `screenshots_per_product` and `screen_rec_per_product` per product. A capture of both products at once uses `product: "ab"` (product A on the left) and counts once toward the capture caps and once toward each product's limit. Every capture names the product and the exact screen, and says what to blur (emails, client names, API keys, billing). Plan only around assets the user said they have, or a screen capturable without a paid account. If the script is clearly better with more, stop at the gate and ask.
- **R-STILL.** `GEN-STILL` is a generated picture that illustrates the line being spoken, capped at `still_cap` per video (opening, offer, a verdict moment, the ending), and never carries a fact: facts go on comparison cards. People and faces are allowed. Each still names a `purpose` of 4 or more words saying what part of the line it shows and a `ken_burns` direction (slow push, a pan, or a pull-out). All the stills in a video share one look so they read as part of the same video. A FLUX prompt names one concrete subject drawn from the scene (a plain, unmarked object or a person at work), three depth layers (a blurred foreground, the subject on a rule-of-thirds point in the midground, a lit background), a bright electric blue background and bright even light, and `16:9`. Props are plain and blank, with no markings, gauges, signs, screens or text. Teal, green, amber, orange, dark, moody, haze, fog and smoke never appear, and no prompt uses a banned prop (R-NOPROPS). Attach `flux_attach`. Describe what is there, never what is absent. No text and no product UI in a generated image.
- **R-GENLIMIT (critical).** Rules in SKILL.md. Detail: Wan clips (`GEN-VIDEO`) go in `gen_role_sections`, in `gen_fill_order`, up to the cap: the hook backdrop, the ending backdrop, then up to two call-to-action backdrops (offer or verdict) when the script needs them. Prompts are one positive sentence within `wan_prompt_words`, end with the required suffix, and describe a scene that fits the line or plain motion; people and faces are allowed. Look: `visual_style`. Hook prompt: strong early motion. Ending prompt: calm, centered, quiet left and right thirds. CTA prompt: open space for the button. Output size and fps are in config `wan`. Clips showing text or a product UI are rejected at review, not edited, and no prompt uses a banned prop (R-NOPROPS). Budget one retry per clip in the RunPod estimate.
- **R-AVATAR** (default generated; skip if mode is none or footage). Rules in SKILL.md. Detail: mark the shots where the avatar shows with `avatar: true`; no avatar run is longer than the in-block cut cadence in SKILL.md. Tier rules apply in full (first-person language reads as personal use).
- **R-PROOF.** Any LINE with a number shows that exact number on screen at the same timestamp, as an `on_screen_text` entry or a `STAT` card. USD, with explicit notation for other currencies. Flag third-party sites that show legacy prices.
- **R-TIER.** hands-on: first-person claims only for listed tested components; demo: describe exactly what the live UI showed; research: no first-person usage anywhere. Every factual claim has a source: own usage (hands-on components only), official pricing/docs, or aggregated review data.
- **R-NOPROPS (critical).** Generated pictures and clips (FLUX stills and Wan clips) never show the props in `banned_prompt_subjects`: coins, piggy banks, clocks, stopwatches, hourglasses, wallets, calculators, dollar signs, banknotes, cash, money, receipts, ledgers and price tags. Cost and time points go on comparison cards, not in pictures. Lint blocks any FLUX or Wan prompt that names one.
- **R-NOFAKE (review).** A generated visual never resembles a named product's real screen, logo or pricing page. Real product visuals come only from supplied `ASSET-SHOT`, `ASSET-CLIP` and `ASSET-BRAND` shots. This is a manual review of every still and clip on a contact sheet, not a word list.
